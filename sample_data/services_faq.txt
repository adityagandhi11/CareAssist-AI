CareAssist Community Clinic - Services and Frequently Asked Questions

This is fictional sample text for demonstrating document search and question answering.

What services does the clinic provide?
The clinic offers general health checkups, preventive wellness visits, routine vaccinations, and administrative referrals. Contact clinic staff to confirm current availability.

Does the clinic provide general health checkups?
Yes. General health checkups are among the services listed in the clinic's sample information. Appointment availability must be confirmed by clinic staff.

What should I bring to an appointment?
Bring a photo ID and your appointment confirmation. If relevant, bring your insurance card and a current medication list.

How do I make an appointment?
Submit a request for an available time through the patient portal. The request is pending until clinic staff approve it.

What are the clinic's hours?
Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM; closed Sundays and public holidays.

Can the AI assistant provide medical advice?
No. The assistant provides administrative information from clinic documents only. It cannot diagnose, recommend treatment, or provide emergency triage. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
