CareAssist Community Clinic - Appointment Policy

This fictional policy is sample content for the CareAssist AI demonstration. It is not a real clinic policy.

Booking
Patients may request an available appointment slot through the patient portal. A request remains pending until approved by clinic staff. Submitting a request does not guarantee an appointment. Clinic staff may contact the patient using the contact details on the account if additional information is needed.

Appointment duration
Routine appointments are scheduled in 30-minute slots. The clinic may adjust availability for operational reasons.

Changes and cancellations
Patients may cancel a future appointment that has not already been completed or cancelled. When possible, provide at least 24 hours' notice. To reschedule, cancel the existing request and submit a new request, or contact clinic staff.

Late arrivals
Patients who expect to arrive late should contact the clinic. Staff may need to reschedule if the delay affects other appointments.

Privacy
Only synthetic patient details should be used in this software demonstration. Never upload real patient records or sensitive personal health information.
